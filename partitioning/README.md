# Possibilities in partitioning of armed conflict types

> Hypothesis: There is an upcoming junction point between entering lasting planetary peace with balanced geographical zones of influence, or instability driven opportunistic continuation of open hostilities in the next technological cycle.

> Hypothesis: Mass adoption of nuclear fusion energy, robotization and resulting wider political involvement and improved conflict resolution will progressively eliminate destructive combat with direct human participation or.

> Hypothesis: Climatic changes and full robotization will lead to new fully-automated industrial zones, separate from urban areas.

## Possible preferred development

Sustained planetary peace is achieved through stability, with (sub-)continentally integrated blocks and balanced cross-block interactions.  With reduced hydrocarbon dependency West Asia gains clear strategic cohesiveness and effectively integrates.  North Eurasia solidarily reconciles systemic resilience against external pressures.  South East Asia avoids open hostilities and further integrates while cohesively coordinating various external influences.  Africa continues towards continental integration, balancing external forces towards stabilization.  Americas further integrate.

Mass inter-(sub)continental trade persists and continues to grow across multilateral zones of influence under regional maritime control.

General realization of macroregional stabilization reduces dichotomy of interests and instability driven conflict build up ...

Hypersonic defense capabilities promote stratosphere into the shared [outer defense zone](../planetary-defense/README.md).

Manned ships with hypersonic launch platforms and laser interception capabilities engage in restrained maneuvering.  Competitive shipping defense leads to non-lethal, non-mutilating, no serious injury boarding engagements.

Ground defense capabilities grow via remotely controlled robotic contests in mechs-only areas and [non-lethal, non-mutilating, no serious injury engagements](../forceful-defense-without-violence/README.md) of influence.

## Possible fallback development

In the case of continued open hostilities, hypersonic carriers could deliver shielded mech platforms.  QE controlled mechs could both defend and threaten hostile takeover of new industrial zones.

Autonomous flotillas could compete for maritime dominance.

Non-lethal, non-mutilating, no serious injury combat for territorial control could be standardized.
