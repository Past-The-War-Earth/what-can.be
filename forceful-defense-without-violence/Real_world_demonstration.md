# Rough Draft Proposal for real world demonstrations

In accordance with [moral values](./Moral_values.md) of forceful defense without violence.

Goal - standardization of the most safe version of tactical, forceful defense without violence between human participants.

A contest for territorial control of a safe, well demarcated area, in safe surroundings. Taking place on a sufficiently firm, grippy, stable, continuous and not very hard surface.  No water bodies may be present in or immediately adjacent to contest area.  No foreign objects or liquid accumulations may be present in the area at the time of a contest, except short grass or alike.  The contest area must be well lit for the duration of the contest.   With a mandatory pre contest, complete and thorough surface check of the demarcated contest area by the below mentioned enforcement authority. 

Involving only willing, well trained in forceful defense without violence, physically and morally prepared individuals of military service age who have been determined to be fit for participation by a medical authority.  Only male participation in direct forceful contact.

Within or between paramilitary or defense-patriotic organizations of a given nation or state.  Within or between military units.  Within or between informal groups provided their mutual disciplined adherence to all rules and sufficient direct oversight.

An observing party with a higher order enforcement authority must be sufficiently present.  This enforcement authority reserves the right to immediately end the contest in case of unmitigated violation of the rules or loss of control.

Contest format proposal for standardization:

1. No charging, one-on-one, in formation combat with hands-only, utilizing only above the belt and below the shoulders hits.
2. Between participants in direct forceful contact being in the same boxing weight class, as measured immediately before equipping the protective items, with weight classes partitioned by unit.
3. All participants in direct forceful contact properly equipped with:
    - padded helmets with face protection,
    - neck projection equipment with heavy padding and full structural protection to ensure no injury to the neck or above or below it,
    - 10 ounce gloves,
    - equivalent to gloves continuous padding of outer and side forearms and elbow,
    - heavily padded belts,
    - very heavily padded, down to below the knee shorts to effectively prevent hits by and to legs and knees,
    - toe to calf muscles, continuous grippy boots with full structural support.
4. With mandatory, pre contest, per participant, complete and thorough equipment checks of the above protective items by the enforcement authority, immediately subsequent to the above mentioned surface check.
5. With a mandatory verification by the enforcement authority of correct equipping of all of the above checked items and no other items by each participant, immediately following the above equipment check.
6. With all participants in direct forceful contact remaining at all times in the demarcated contest area in visibly separated, by weight class groups, in clear sight of the enforcement authority.

No mechanical implements in or around the contest area.  No equipment in the contest area besides safety gear and impact dampening implements. Sufficiently padded body prosthetics are allowed.

Additional participant specializations:

1. One older, more experienced director per 3 active defenders,  in supervising function only.  
2. One supervising coordinator and one coordinator assistant per 3 directors.
3. 2 or more embedded medics under each supervising coordinator.
4. One morale, one physical and one tactical supervisor per 3 coordinators, for up to 3 unit levels above in the unit hierarchy.
5. 3 or more communications runners per coordinator unit and above.
6. Up to one headquarters with up to 5 members present within the contested territory.

Some reserved defenders per director's unit and/or above units.

No weapons in or around the contest area.  Directors are allowed to use padded long staff for the sole purpose of longer-range touch communication with their team.

...

Professional medical personnel with ability for rapid evacuation present and ready to aid contestants.  Upon any occurence of a serious injury contests end immediately.
