# Rough draft of an implementation of Non-Lethal, Non-Mutilating, No Serious Injury combat 

[Moral values](./Moral_values.md) driven Non-Lethal, Non-Mutilating, No Serious Injury combat outside of the boundaries of controlled non-technological contests. With the aid of technological implements ensuring all combatants being alive, whole and undamaged.  With mandatory observation by a neutral party with a higher-order moral authority.

Requires:
- Development of technology necessary to create "smart" armor capable of individual combatant or combatant group movement adjustments without blocking tactical goals.
- Development of adhesive projectile technology and the associated anti-adhesive technology.
- Successful [real-world demonstrations](./Real_world_demonstration.md) of forceful defense without violence.

One-on-one, padded-glove, in-formation combat, taking place between medium armored junior enlisted men with exposed front torso and upper arms, possibly equipping shields.  Medium armored with passively armored release/throwing arm adhesive projectile force may participate at a safe distance without entering into direct combat.  Armor and mandatory face-paint must fully repel adhesive projectiles.  At least 2 fully medium armored medics must be present per squad.

Directing enlisted men are fully light armored non-commissioned officers wielding staff or alike.  One non-commissioned officer per team of 3 junior enlisted men and two non-commissioned officers per squad of 3 teams.  Combat between opposing non-commissioned officer corps is meant to prevent combat between junior enlisted men. 

One morale officer, one tactical officer and one health officer per 3 squads, and so up the command chain.  Junior officers are to be fully heavily armored and are to wield blunted swords. Combat between opposing junior officer corps is meant to prevent combat between enlisted men and to take place before combat of non-commissioned officers.

Mechanically mounted and fully medium armored senior officer corps and headquarters personnel are to wield hollow, rounded off lances.  Combat between opposing mounted officer corps is meant to prevent combat between enlisted men and to take place before any other form of combat.

Mixed combat involving officers and enlisted men is for training purposes only.

Forceful defense without violence principles are to be upheld in all forms of combat against all types of opponents.  All measures must be taken to ensure that everyone is alive, whole and not seriously injured.  Utmost care, discipline and professionalism must be applied in armed combat.  All armor, including helmets, must be fully and properly equipped by all combatants at all times during their participation in direct combat.  Armor must effectively prevent damage from weapons and forceful impacts, above all projecting head, spine, body's vital systems and body's structural integrity.
