# What Can Be in Defense

- **Defense** - *The action of defending from or resisting attack*

The need for defense is self evident on every level of existence.

- **Violence** - *Behavior involving physical force intended to hurt, damage, or kill someone or something*

Killing, mutilating or causing serious injury to people is  wrong. Always.

Question: Is violence necessary in defense?  Can violence be limited or even eliminated in defense?

Answer: We can limit violence if all parties in armed conflict mutually agree to do so.  Sufficient control can eliminate violence in defense.


- **War** - *A state of armed conflict between different nations or states or different groups within a nation or state*

Wars happen for cultural, territorial or economic reasons.  Economic reasons are the most dynamic and least controllable.

> Hypothesis: War <a href="./partitioning/README.md">can be partitioned</a> into non-destructive and destructive.  Direct human participation can be limited to non-destructive war.

> For example, blunt impact + adhesive projectile territorial control combat by humans.  Autonomous mech combat in industrial areas.


## Effective defense with force without violence

We can successfully defend by force without killing, mutilation or seriously injuring.

Question: Where is it hardest?

Answer: Where we use force capable of killing, mutilating or seriously injuring.

- **Arms** - *Weapons and ammunition; armaments* - military equipment

Armor can be "smart" and prevent death, mutilation or serious injury.  Weapons can adhere to the Alive, Whole and Undamaged principle.

> For example, mechanized or adaptive armor can prevent body part positions that lead or are vulnerable to heavy blows. Boxing gloves for soldiers, impact-dampening staff for sergeants officers.

### Is this possible?

Yes, if we have enough control when needed.

> Hypothesis: Arrival of nuclear fusion energy, robotization and resulting abundance of resources will reduce economic reasons for war and allow for properly controlled warfare.

### How

#### 3 component system

* **Conflict Prevention** - prevent conflict by finding solutions that work for all involved parties

* **War Prevention** - demonstrate mutual will to prevent war

* **Controlled Combat** - control the use of force in combat

#### Development Sequence

Demonstrate then implement in steps.

##### Demonstrate

First demonstrate that until we have sufficient control we can:

1. **Control war**

First, the point of greatest impact - demonstrate [forceful defense without violence](./forceful-defense-without-violence/README.md)

Get the peace of mind that if war happens everyone will come home alive, in one piece and without serious injury.

2. **Prevent war**

Second, show [the will to prevent war](./will-to-prevent-war/README.md).

Using the adversarial cooperation mechanisms developed for the first component, develop data aggregation mechanisms and show collective will to resolve the built up tensions in a better way.

...

3. **Prevent conflict**

Third, demonstrate the ability to [resolve tensions](./preventing-conflict/README.md) before they build into a crisis.

Using already developed mechanisms and subsequently developed regulation based, cooperative and deterministic, automated reasoning mechanisms, make decisions mindful of our tensions.

...

##### Implement

Then implement this solution in steps:

1. Implement forceful defense without violence, in-step with modern development of warfare, with heavy reliance on conventional technology. Aid economic development while channeling the direction of armor and weapons development.  Maintain governable stability.

2. After establishment of forceful defense without violence,  channel it towards fostering moral values over technological superiority.

3. With sufficient passage of time and development of parallel vectors for [planetary defense](./planetary-defense/README.md) reduce technological dominance in non-destructive human combat.

> Many centuries from now, before full establishment of planetary peace, man-to-man combat should be weaponless with safety gear, overseen by elders.  It is meant  to resolve the remaining differences left after the "who is right" questions have been debated.

Let's not arrive at a situation where combatants without proper gear face death or permanent injury from a staff blow.

Elements of organized physical defense may remain in culture past the accomplishment of world peace, likely reducing in scale and jurisdiction.
